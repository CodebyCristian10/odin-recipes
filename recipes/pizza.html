Pizza

Description

A crispy, golden crust topped with tangy tomato sauce, melted cheese, and your favorite toppings — simple, customizable, and always satisfying.

Ingredients

For the dough:

  • 300 g (2½ cups) all-purpose or bread flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeastli
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 200 ml (¾ cup + 1 tbsp) warm water

For the topping:

  • 1/2 cup pizza or tomato sauce
  • 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Toppings of choice (pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, olives, etc.)
  • Fresh basil (optional)
  • Olive oil, for drizzling

Step by Step

  1. Mix the dough: In a large bowl, mix 300 grams all-purpose or bread flour, 1 teaspoons instant yeast, and 1 teaspoons salt. Add 1 tablespoons olive oil and 200 milliliters warm water, and stir until a shaggy dough forms.
  2. Knead the dough: Knead the dough on a floured surface for 10 minutes 10:00 until smooth and elastic.
  3. Let the dough rise: Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot until doubled in size.
  4. Preheat oven: Preheat the oven to 250°C (480°F), or as high as it goes. If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven now.
  5. Shape the dough: Punch down the dough and shape it into a 12-inch round on a floured surface or piece of parchment paper.
  6. Add sauce and toppings: Spread 0.5 cups pizza or tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust. Sprinkle 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella cheese on top, then add 1 cups toppings of choice (pepperoni, mushrooms, peppers, olives, etc.).
  7. Bake the pizza: Bake for 12 minutes 12:00 , until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
  8. Garnish and serve: Remove from the oven, top with 5 fresh basil leaves, and drizzle with 1 tablespoons olive oil, for drizzling. Slice and serve.

Notes

For a crispier crust, preheat a pizza stone or an upside-down baking sheet in the oven. Let the dough come to room temperature before shaping if it was refrigerated.
