Fideos

Description

The fideos are an excellent dish if you want to eat something quick and delicious.

Below, I'll show you how to make them.

Ingredients

  • 250g of fideos
  • 2 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 onion. chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon oregano or basil
  • Grated parmesan cheese
  • Water, for boiling

Step by Step

  1. Boil water: Bring 2 liters water, for boiling to a boil in a large pot. Add a pinch of 0.5 teaspoons salt.
  2. Cook the noodles: Add the 250 grams noodles (spaghetti or preferred type) to the boiling water and cook according to package instructions, usually 9 minutes 09:00 , until al dente.
  3. Drain and set aside: Drain the noodles, reserving a small cup of the pasta water. Set the noodles aside.
  4. Sauté the aromatics: In a large pan, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil over medium heat. Add 2 garlic cloves, minced and 0.5 onion, chopped, and sauté for 3 minutes 03:00 until fragrant and translucent.
  5. Add the tomato: Add 1 tomato, chopped to the pan and cook for another 4 minutes 04:00 , stirring occasionally, until softened.
  6. 6 Combine noodles and sauce: Add the cooked noodles to the pan. Toss well to coat, adding a splash of the reserved pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
  7. Season and serve: Season with 0.5 teaspoons salt, 0.3 teaspoons black pepper, and 1 teaspoons oregano or basil. Toss again, then plate and top with 2 tablespoons grated parmesan cheese.
