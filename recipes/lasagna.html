Lasagna

Description

A rich, comforting bake of layered pasta, seasoned meat sauce, and melted cheese — hearty, cheesy, and perfect for feeding a crowd.

Ingredients

For the meat sauce:

  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 onion chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 700g crushed tomatoes
  • 2tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

For the cheese layer:

  • 250g ricota cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cup grated parmesan cheese

Other:

  • 9-12 lasagna noodles
  • Fresh parsley or basil, for garnish (optional)

Step by Step

  1. Sauté the aromatics: Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add 1 onion, chopped and 2 garlic cloves, minced, and cook for 3 minutes 03:00 until softened.
  2. Brown the meat: Add 500 grams ground beef (or beef and pork mix) to the pot and cook, breaking it apart, until browned, about 7 minutes 07:00 .
  3. Simmer the sauce: Stir in 700 grams crushed tomatoes or tomato sauce, 2 tablespoons tomato paste, 1 teaspoons dried oregano, 1 teaspoons dried basil, 1 teaspoons salt, and 0.5 teaspoons black pepper. Simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
  4. Cook the noodles: If using regular noodles, cook 10 lasagna noodles (or no-boil sheets) in salted boiling water according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside. Skip this step if using no-boil sheets.
  5. Make the cheese mixture: In a bowl, mix 250 grams ricotta cheese, 1 large eggs, and half of the 0.5 cups grated parmesan cheese until well combined.
  6. Preheat oven: Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
  7. Assemble the layers: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish. Layer noodles, then ricotta mixture, then meat sauce, then a sprinkle of 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers, finishing with sauce and a generous layer of 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese and remaining 0.5 cups grated parmesan cheese on top.
  8. Bake the lasagna: Cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes 45:00 . Remove the foil and bake for another 15-20 minutes, until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  9. Rest and serve: Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes 15:00 before slicing. Garnish with 2 fresh parsley or basil, for garnish and serve.
